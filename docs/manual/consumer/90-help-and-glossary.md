# Part 6 - Everyday problems and word list

## Everyday problems

| What you see | Likely reason | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A page says the subscription is inactive | Your shop's paid period has ended | Contact PosBuzz support to renew, then click **Retry** on the page. Staff can sign in again as soon as a new period starts. |
| A page says it cannot be found | The address is mistyped, or the account does not exist | Check the address. It should be your shop name followed by the PosBuzz domain. |
| You cannot sign in | Wrong email or password, or too many attempts in a minute | Check the details. Wait a minute before trying again. Ask the owner to reset your password if you forgot it. |
| A menu item described in this manual is missing | Your role does not include that permission, or your plan does not include the feature | Ask the owner or a manager with the *Users & Roles* permission. |
| A list does not show a change you just made | The screen is showing older data | Click the circular arrow in the top bar (tooltip "Clear Application Cache"). It loads the lists again and does not sign you out. Or reload the page. |
| You want to read the manual again | Not applicable | Click the book icon ("User Guide (PDF)") in the top bar. The manual opens as a PDF in a new tab. |
| After you delete a role, the people who had it are gone from Users | Deleting a role in use also removed those people in our test | Before deleting a role, move its people to another role in Settings > Users. |
| You open a page and are sent to the sign-in page | Your session ended | Sign in again. |
| A sale is refused with a message about stock | The batch has fewer items than you tried to sell | Lower the quantity, pick another batch, or add stock. Check **Stock Overview**. |
| A sale is refused with a message about a price change | A price was edited while the sale was open | Refresh the product list on the screen, add the item again and complete the sale. |
| The receipt does not print and a warning appears | The print program on the shop computer is not running | The sale is already saved. Start the print program (Part 5, printing section) and reprint from the sale's page. |
| Something you tried says an item "already exists" | A customer, vendor, brand or similar with that name or phone number is already saved | Search for it and use the existing one. |
| A message says an item is "still used by other records" | You tried to delete something that products, sales or stock still refer to | Keep it, or make it inactive where the screen offers that. |
| The page looks wrong after an update | Your browser still has the old version | Reload the page. If it still looks wrong, close the app and open it again. |

## When you need help

1. Open **Support** in the left menu (if you do not see it, you do not have the permission).
2. Raise a ticket and describe what happened. Part 1 explains the form.
3. PosBuzz staff reply inside the ticket. You will see the answer on the same page.

Include the screen name, what you clicked, the exact message you saw and roughly when it happened. A photo of the screen is even better.

## Word list

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Batch | One delivery of one product, with its own quantity, cost, price and optional expiry date. |
| Branch | A shop, outlet or warehouse of yours. Stock is kept per branch. |
| Consumer | The word PosBuzz staff use for your shop account. |
| Due | An amount a customer still owes you, or that you still owe a vendor. |
| Inventory | The stock you hold, kept as batches. |
| Permission | One thing a user is allowed to do or see. |
| Plan | The package your shop subscribes to. It sets which features are available. |
| Promotion | A discount rule that can apply automatically or be chosen at the till. |
| Quotation | A price offer for a customer. It does not change your stock until it is turned into a sale. |
| Return | Items a customer brings back, with a refund or an exchange. |
| Role | A named set of permissions given to staff. |
| Subdomain | The first part of your shop's address, for example `myshop` in `myshop.posbuzz.xyz`. |
| Unit | How a product is counted or sold, for example piece, kilogram or pack. |
| Variant | One version of a product, such as a size or colour, with its own price and stock. |
| Vendor | A supplier you buy from. |
