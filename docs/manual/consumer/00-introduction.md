# Welcome to PosBuzz

PosBuzz is the point-of-sale system for your shop. With it you can:

- ring up sales at the till and print receipts,
- keep track of your products, stock, vendors and what you owe them,
- take returns, give quotations and collect money that customers still owe,
- manage online orders and the couriers who deliver them,
- reward loyal customers with points,
- see how the day and the month went, and
- give each member of staff only the screens they need.

This manual explains every screen of the Shop App, step by step. You can open it at any time from inside the app: click the **book icon** ("User Guide (PDF)") in the top bar. It opens this manual as a PDF in a new browser tab. You do not need to read it from start to finish. Use the **Contents** page to jump to the screen you need.

## Signing in for the first time

1. Open the address of your shop. It is your shop name followed by the PosBuzz domain, for example `myshop.posbuzz.xyz`. PosBuzz staff choose this address when they create your shop account, and your PosBuzz contact gives it to you. The first part of the address (your subdomain) is also printed on your PosBuzz billing invoice. If you do not know the address, ask your PosBuzz contact or the shop owner. Save it as a bookmark in your browser so you can open it quickly next time.
2. Enter the email and password you were given.
3. Press **Login**.

If you see a page saying your subscription is inactive, your shop's paid period has ended. Contact PosBuzz support to renew it. Nobody in your shop can sign in until it is renewed.

## What you may see depends on your role

The shop owner decides what each person can do. Every person has a **role**, and every role has a list of **permissions**. The left-hand menu only shows the screens your role allows. If you cannot find a screen described in this manual, you probably do not have the permission for it. Ask the owner or a manager with the *Users & Roles* permission to change your role.

Your shop's **plan** also limits what is available. A feature that is not included in your plan does not appear for anyone in the shop.

## Conventions used in this manual

- Names of buttons, fields, menu items and messages appear in **bold**, exactly as the app shows them.
- A menu path such as **Sales > View Sales** means: open **Sales** in the left menu, then click **View Sales**.
- Tables headed *Field | Required | Notes* list every box on a form. A red star in the app marks a required box.
- Tables headed *Message | What it means | What to do* list the messages the app shows when something needs your attention.
- A yellow note starting with **Good to know:** describes something that may surprise you.
- Amounts are in Bangladeshi taka. Dates and times use Bangladesh time (Asia/Dhaka). A business day runs from midnight to midnight in that time zone.

## Using the app on a phone or tablet

The Shop App adjusts to the screen. On a phone the menu folds away: tap the menu button at the top left to open it, tap an item, and it closes again. Tables scroll sideways. Forms open as panels that fill the screen. The Point of Sale screen works on a tablet or computer best, because it shows many things at once.

You can also install the app from your browser's menu (for example **Add to Home screen** on a phone, or the install icon in the address bar on a computer) so that it opens like a normal app.

## Before you start selling: a checklist for a new shop

Follow this order the first time. Each step points to the part of the manual that explains it.

1. Check your **Company Profile** (name, address, logo). *Part 1.*
2. Create your **Branches** if you have more than one place. *Part 1.*
3. Create **Roles** and **Users** for your staff. *Part 1.*
4. Add **Units**, **Categories**, **Brands** and **Tags**. *Part 2.*
5. Add your **Vendors**. *Part 3.*
6. Add your **Products**. *Part 2.*
7. Add your starting stock with **Add Inventory**. *Part 3.*
8. Add your **Customers** (or add them as you sell). *Part 5.*
9. Set up **Printer Settings** and the receipt printer. *Part 5.*
10. Make a test sale on the **Point of Sale** screen and check the receipt. *Part 4.*
